How it works

  1. You send the request. Nothing is charged yet.
  2. We check the dates with the owner and send you the deposit details by GCash or Maya.
  3. Your booking is confirmed once the deposit is received. The owner's number is sent then.
  4. You pay the balance to the owner at pickup. Bring a valid driver's license for self-drive.
